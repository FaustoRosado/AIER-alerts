○ Evidence: Lifecycle policies, Glue job logs, sample Athena queries, retention validation

● Automate Aggressively: Manual processes don't scale. Automate responses, remediation, and reporting. 
● Compliance Early: Starting compliance mapping now makes Sprint 4-5 easier and demonstrates maturity. 
● Security Testing: Find vulnerabilities before your evaluators do. Document findings and fixes. 
● Document as You Build: Don't wait until the end of the sprint to document. Capture screenshots and logs as you work.
